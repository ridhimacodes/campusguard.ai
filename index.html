Frequently Asked Questions

Is CampusGuard.AI completely free to use?

Yes! The core Student Plan is 100% free forever for all individual college students. You can scan PG bills, verify internship offers, and track college notices without adding any card details.

How does CampusGuard verify DISCOM electricity tariffs?

We scrape official public state regulatory commission tariff schedules (like DERC, KERC, TNERC) and update them in real time so our comparisons reflect actual legitimate slab rates.

Does CampusGuard provide legal representation?

No. CampusGuard provides informational data research and consumer rights education based on publicly accessible rules and notices. It does not replace formal legal counsel.
